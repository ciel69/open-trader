# Code of Conduct

Be respectful and constructive. We want a welcoming project for everyone regardless of background or experience.

- Discuss ideas and code, not people. No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or doxxing.
- Assume good faith; give specific, kind feedback; accept it the same way.
- Do not share anyone's private data (keys, balances, trade logs, personal paths).
- No promotion of financial schemes, signals for sale, or pressure to trade with real money.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this and may block repeat offenders.
Report problems privately to the contact listed in `SECURITY.md` (placeholder until the maintainer fills it in).

Adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant 2.1 (https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
